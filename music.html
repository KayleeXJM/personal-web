As a musician

Drums

Self-taught. I've always loved beats and rhythms, the kind that get into your body before your brain has a chance to overthink. Two months before graduation I learned to play drums through online videos, joined a band of friends, and performed at the graduation show. Being on stage with people you love turns out to be the easiest kind of brave.

♫

Flute

Six years of formal study starting in primary school, when my school offered art electives and I chose the flute. I don't play much anymore, but the instrument shaped how I hear melody — and I still have a deep soft spot for its sound.

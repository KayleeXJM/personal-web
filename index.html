Hello, I'm 徐佳茗

Kaylee Xu

I'm currently an M.S. student in Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon University, graduating in December 2026. Before CMU, I studied Data Science and Finance at NYU Shanghai, where I developed a strong foundation in machine learning, statistics, and software engineering.

Over the past few years, I've worked on AI systems at Pinterest, TikTok, and TCL Technology, spanning recommendation systems, multimodal learning, and large language models. These experiences have shaped my interests in user modeling, personalization, sequential behavior modeling, and LLM-powered AI systems. More broadly, I'm interested in building AI that better understands humans — not only what people say or do, but how their preferences, intentions, and goals evolve over time.

I’m currently seeking full-time MLE roles starting January 2027. Whether you’re hiring or just want to talk about ML — I’m always happy to connect.

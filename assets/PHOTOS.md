# Photo Placeholders

Add your images here. The website will load them automatically once in place.

## Hero photo
`assets/hero.jpg`
- Recommended: natural, candid — not a headshot
- Aspect ratio: 3:4 (portrait) works best

To enable it, open `index.html` and replace the `photo-placeholder` div in the
Hero section with:
```html
<img src="assets/hero.jpg" alt="Kaylee" />
```
